### 🌿 TIER 2 — Responsive & Interactive
*Everything in Tier 1, plus:*

- **Full Cross-Device Engineering** — optimized for foldables, tablets, and ultra-wide displays
- **Mobile Touch UX** — swipe gestures, slide-out navigation drawer, bottom-sheet modals
- **Cinematic Video Hero** — looping background video on the homepage
- **Seasonal Promo Banner** — staff-managed header banner for holidays and events
- **Interactive Park Map** — clickable points of interest with photos, prices, and booking buttons
- **Mega Menu Navigation** — 5-column desktop popover, mobile accordion
- **Wyattel Food Delivery Integration** — ordering workflow direct to hotel guests
- **Priority Notifications** — instant Gmail alerts for urgent bookings and inquiries
- **Custom Scrollbar UI** — proximity-glow interactive indicator

---

### 🌳 TIER 3 — Ultimate Enterprise
*Everything in Tier 2, plus:*

- **Payment Gateway** — GCash, Maya, Credit/Debit Cards, QR Ph via PayMongo / Xendit

- **Real-Time Reservations** — live availability calendar for Verde Villa, Cottages, and Function Hall
- **Dual Calendar Sync** — all bookings auto-sync to Google Calendar + Google Sheets
- **QR Gate Scanner** — downloadable e-tickets that staff scan at the entrance for check-in
- **Cost Estimator** — self-service quotation builder for weddings, birthdays, and corporate events
- **Wyattel & Tourism Partner Portal** — bundled "Stay & Tour" packages and regional attraction guides
- **Live Reviews Wall** — auto-synced Google and Facebook guest ratings and guest photo social wall

# SLIDE 7 — DESIGN DIRECTION

## The Look & Feel We Are Going For

> *Warm. Natural. Elevated. Unmistakably Qetsiyah.*

**Visual Identity:**
- Rich olive greens, warm creams, and earthy terracottas — reflecting the park's tropical landscape
- Cinematic full-bleed photography and video — letting the park sell itself
- Elegant serif typography for headings, clean sans-serif for body — a luxury eco-resort feel
- Generous white space — giving content room to breathe

**Interaction Design:**
- Smooth scroll animations and section transitions
- Teleprompter-style text reveals as guests scroll
- Carousel image sliders with infinite loop
- Hover effects that feel alive and responsive

**Responsive:**
- Designed first for mobile, perfected for desktop
- Tested on all screen sizes — no cut-offs, no horizontal scroll
