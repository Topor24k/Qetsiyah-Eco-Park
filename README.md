## 📖 About This Project

The **Qetsiyah Eco Park Web Platform** is the official digital gateway and interactive guest experience for **Qetsiyah Eco Park**, a premier eco-tourism, recreation, and private event destination in the heart of Sultan Kudarat.

This project was conceived and engineered to translate the peaceful, lush atmosphere of the physical park into a world-class digital experience. It bridges hospitality, storytelling, and modern web technology — giving guests, families, event organizers, and travelers a seamless way to explore what the park has to offer, plan their visit, and book their occasions.

---

## 🎯 The Purpose & Problem We Solve

Before this platform, potential guests relied primarily on informal social media inquiries, where questions about operating hours, activity rates, dining menus, and venue availability often went unanswered or took hours to resolve.

This project directly addresses these challenges by providing:

1. **A Definitive Digital Destination**: A fast, mobile-first single source of truth for all park information, directions, safety guidelines, and announcements.
2. **Transparent Trip Planning**: Prospective visitors can view real video footage of attractions, browse full restaurant menus with pricing, and assess venue facilities before arriving.
3. **Frictionless Booking & Reservations**: A structured reservation inquiry system that collects guest counts, desired dates, preferred services, and specific ride interests in a single, organized step.
4. **Authentic Storytelling**: Elevating the park beyond just a commercial facility by celebrating its origins, its dedicated local staff, culinary artists, and the welcoming community that makes Qetsiyah feel like home.
5. **Regional Tourism Catalyst**: Positioned not just as an isolated venue, but as a flagship attraction driving tourism and economic vitality into Tacurong City and neighboring communities.

## 🌟 Core Digital Experiences

### 1. Immersive Cinematic Storytelling
- **High-Definition Video Heroes**: Direct video streams embedded in the opening sections (Activities and Dining) that give visitors an immediate, living sense of the park's energy and natural beauty.
- **Teleprompter Narrative Scroll**: A slow-paced, meditative text reveal that invites visitors to pause, read, and connect with the park's founding spirit.
- **Rotating Showcase Carousel**: An infinite multi-chapter story covering *The Place*, *The Community*, *The Experiences*, *The Hospitality*, and *The Future*, with rotating photography highlighting the people behind the scenes.

### 2. Comprehensive Park Activities Catalog
Showcases all major park attractions with transparent descriptions, track specifications, safety guidelines, and direct booking links:
- **Zip Lining** — High-velocity scenic aerial line over tropical foliage.
- **Sky Biking** — Suspended high-wire dual-track bicycle crossing.
- **Freshwater Paddle Boats** — Leisure cruising on the park's tranquil lagoon.
- **Scenic Horse Riding** — Guided nature trails accompanied by expert caretakers.
- **Open-Air Playground** — Safe, shaded lawn recreation for children.
- **Kiddy Splash Pool** — Shallow, crystal-clear wading pool for young families.

### 3. Digital Culinary Experience
An editorial-style, print-inspired digital menu for **Qetsiyah Café & Dining**:
- Complete menu offerings spanning Combo Meals, Soup Dishes, Sizzling & Grill, Chicken & Beef Specialties, Pasta, Snacks, and Refreshing Beverages.
- Equal-tile high-contrast photography previews on the homepage that drive appetite and interest.

### 4. Interactive Booking & Inquiry Engine
A centralized reservation form capturing complete guest intent:
- Guest contact details and group headcounts.
- Target visit dates and scheduling.
- Selected package type (Day Tour, Venue Rental, Villa Stay).
- Multi-select attraction checklist so staff can prepare equipment and tickets in advance.

### 5. Curated Photo Mosaic Gallery
A responsive editorial photo gallery categorized into distinct visitor themes sourced from authentic park moments:
- *Scenery & Atmosphere*
- *Events & Gatherings*
- *Attractions & Activities*
- *Amenities & Visitor Life*
- *Community & Staff*
- *Culture & Regional Celebrations*

### 6. Regional Hospitality Gateway
Features direct partnerships with downtown accommodations, starting with **Wyattel Hotel (Tacurong City)**, ensuring guests arriving from outside the province have a complete tour-and-stay solution.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy & Brand Identity

The digital design reflects the identity of Qetsiyah: **warm, organic, grounded, and dignified.**

- **Warm Natural Palette**:
  - *Amber Gold (`#df9039`)* — Radiant sunlight, energy, and primary calls-to-action.
  - *Olive Forest Dark (`#383e24`)* — Mature canopy trees, tranquility, and grounding contrast.
  - *Warm Parchment & Aged Paper (`#eae3d4`, `#f3ede2`)* — Editorial sophistication that replaces harsh digital whites with gentle organic tones.
- **Classic Editorial Typography**:
  - *Playfair Display* — A high-contrast transitional serif that lends authority, grace, and timeless elegance to display headlines.
  - *Plus Jakarta Sans* — A crisp geometric sans-serif delivering effortless legibility across desktop screens and mobile displays.
- **Motion with Purpose**:
  - Smooth 60fps easing loops and gentle fades that guide the eye without overwhelming the senses.
  - Mobile app-style bottom navigation bar giving smartphone users immediate, thumb-friendly access to Home, Explore, Dining, Gallery, and Booking.

## 💻 Technical Architecture Summary

- **Frontend Core**: React 18 Single Page Application (SPA) with zero-refresh hash routing.
- **Tooling & Compilation**: Vite 6 for lightning-fast module bundling and production assets.
- **Styling Architecture**: Pure, semantic CSS with custom design tokens (`var(--...)`) — lightweight, performant, and dependency-free.
- **Performance & Accessibility**: Asynchronous media decoding, responsive picture element sets, SVG iconography via Lucide, and full keyboard/screen-reader friendliness.
